Routines

Pre-defined, automated sequences of agentic actions that trigger on a schedule or an event. The "Set and Forget" of AI agency.

General Use Case

The Digital Morning Paper: Every day at 7 AM, Claude scans your emails, calendar, and news feeds to generate a prioritized 1-page briefing on your desk.

Productivity Use Case

Automatic Expense Auditor: Weekly scanning of PDFs/receipts in a folder, categorization, and updating a master financial spreadsheet.

Dispatch

Intelligent task routing. Dispatch allows a primary agent to analyze a complex request and delegate sub-tasks to a swarm of specialized agents.

General Use Case

The Personal COO: Request a complex trip to Tokyo. Dispatch routes tasks to a Flight Agent, a Hotel Agent, and a Cultural Guide Agent, merging their results into one itinerary.

Project Use Case

Content Engine: One prompt triggers a researcher, a writer, an editor, and a social media strategist to produce a full campaign across 4 platforms.

Dreams

Background synthesis. Dreams allow the AI to process information, simulate outcomes, and refine ideas while the user is inactive.

General Use Case

The Creative Catalyst: Give Claude a mood board and a rough goal. While you sleep, "Dreams" synthesize these into three distinct, fully-fleshed-out brand strategies.

Research Use Case

Pattern Discovery: Over a weekend, Claude analyzes 1,000 customer feedback entries to identify subtle, non-obvious trends that a human might miss.
